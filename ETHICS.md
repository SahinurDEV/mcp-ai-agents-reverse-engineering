# Ethics & Legal Use

The reverse engineering material in this repository is for **education on targets you are allowed to analyze**:

- the course CrackMes in `s15-crackmes/` (written for this course),
- crackmes.one challenges, within that site's rules,
- your own programs, APIs and MCP servers,
- open-source software, within its license.

Do **not** use these techniques to bypass licensing, DRM or anti-cheat, to break into systems you do not own, or to analyze malware outside an isolated lab. Report vulnerabilities through responsible disclosure. Treat MCP tool descriptions and tool results as untrusted input.

By using the RE sections you agree to these rules.
